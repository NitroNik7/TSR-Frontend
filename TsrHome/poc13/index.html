Trading with Overbought / sold Oscillators

While these indicators are time-tested and powerful, incorrect usage can lead to significant losses. To use them effectively, consider the following key strategies:

  1. Identify the Right Indicator: Select tools that match your specific use case and trading style.
  2. Understand Market Type: Determine if the market is trending or moving sideways.
  3. Entry & Exit Points as indicated in charts will give better results
  4. Avoid Isolated Use: Do not trade based on these levels alone.
  5. Monitor Momentum: Use indicators to gauge momentum (e.g., observing a rise over the last four days).
  6. Incorporate Support and Resistance: Combine these indicators with recent support and resistance levels for better accuracy.

They are used for

  • Identifying Reversals
  • Spotting Divergence
  • Trend Confirmation
  • Entry and Exit Timing
